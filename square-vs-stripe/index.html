    The short answer

    • Mostly online (web store, invoices, payment links): Stripe usually costs less than Square Free. Square Plus closes the gap for $49 a month.
    • Mostly at a counter: Square is the simpler choice. The fees are close, its point-of-sale app is free, and the hardware is made to work with it.
    • Both: some businesses use Square at the counter and Stripe online. It saves money once online sales are large, at the cost of two systems, two payouts and two sets of reports.

    Beyond the fees

    • Who it’s built for. Square is a point-of-sale system that also takes payments online. Stripe is a payments platform that your website, invoicing or booking software plugs into. If nobody on your team will set up software, that alone may decide it.
    • Getting your money. Square pays out the next business day for free. Stripe takes about two business days, and its very first payout 7 to 14 days.
    • Chargebacks. Square charges nothing to handle a dispute; Stripe charges $15, or $30 if you contest it and lose. For businesses that see disputes often, that adds up.
    • Moving later. Neither has a contract. Saved customer cards and subscriptions are the sticky part of moving between any two providers, so decide before you have many.
