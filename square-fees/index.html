    Where the rates come from. Current rates were read from Square’s US pricing page on November 18, 2026: Free 2.6% + 15¢ in person, 3.3% + 30¢ online, 3.5% + 15¢ keyed; Plus ($49/mo) 2.5% + 15¢ and 2.9% + 30¢; Premium ($149/mo) 2.4% + 15¢ and 2.9% + 30¢. The “before January” Free-plan figures (2.9% + 30¢ online, 2.6% + 10¢ in person) come from third-party reporting, because Square’s page shows only today’s rates. Stripe’s standard online rate, 2.9% + 30¢, was read from stripe.com/pricing the same day.

    What this leaves out. Custom or negotiated rates, hardware, instant-transfer fees, Afterpay, ACH, international cards, chargebacks, and everything a paid plan includes beyond its rates. It compares processing cost only, so it’s a starting point, not advice to switch plans or providers. Not affiliated with Square or Stripe; nobody pays for a recommendation here.
