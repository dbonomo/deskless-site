Square vs Clover: which costs less for your business?

Clover’s Growth plans have the lowest published in-person rate, 2.3% + 10¢, but they come with monthly software, hardware and often a contract. Square has no monthly fee and no contract, and costs less on online orders. Put in a month of sales to see which wins for you.

    The short answer

    • Small or new business, mostly at a counter or on the go: Square. No monthly fee, no contract, free point-of-sale app, next-day payouts at no charge.
    • Busy counter with steady volume, staying put for years: Clover’s Growth plans charge 2.3% + 10¢ in person, the lowest published counter rate of the three, but with $84.95 a month in software, real hardware cost, and often a contract. Run your numbers above.
    • Lots of online or phone orders: Clover charges its typed-in rate, 3.5% + 10¢, on every card-not-present sale. On orders over about $33, Square Plus (or Stripe) costs less; Square Free beats it over $100.

    Beyond the fees

    • Leaving. Square has no contract; you can stop using it tomorrow. With Clover, the hardware subscription can’t be cancelled, promotions require a 3-year contract, and termination fees depend on your provider.
    • Who you call. Square’s support is Square. Clover’s support often runs through whoever sold it to you, which can be great (a local bank rep) or a maze.
    • Hardware. Clover devices are generally tied to the processor that sold them. Square hardware works only with Square. Either way, the hardware is part of the decision.
