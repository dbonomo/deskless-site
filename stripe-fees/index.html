    Every Stripe fee, in one table

    Stripe’s standard US pricing has no monthly fee, setup fee or cancellation fee. You pay per payment, plus extras for some kinds of payment and some services.

    Three things that surprise people

    • Invoices cost twice. A card payment on a Stripe invoice pays the card rate and the Invoicing fee. On a $2,000 invoice, that’s $58.30 for the card plus $8 for Invoicing Starter. If you’re only sending a bill, a Payment Link avoids the Invoicing fee.
    • The first payout takes a while. After your first live payment, Stripe’s first payout typically arrives 7 to 14 days later. After that it’s about two business days.
    • Losing a dispute you fought costs $30. $15 when it arrives, and another $15 if you submit evidence; that second $15 comes back only if you win. Square, by comparison, charges no dispute fee.

    Is Stripe cheaper than Square?

    Online, usually yes, since Square raised its Free-plan online rate to 3.3% + 30¢ in January 2026. At the counter, Square is the simpler choice: its point-of-sale app is free, and Stripe needs a separate one. The calculator above shows both for your own numbers, and Square vs Stripe goes through the differences beyond price.
