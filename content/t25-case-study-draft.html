REF · CASE-01

Vending Vault — full-service vending machine provider, Northern Colorado.

Challenge: Vending Vault's pitch — free installation, weekly service, no contracts — is a real differentiator, but the old stock WordPress site didn't reflect it and was slow for the owner to update. Customers standing at a jammed machine had no fast way to report it.

Approach: Dan rebuilt the site on Astro and Tailwind with Decap CMS, so the owner edits photos and copy himself — no developer needed. SEO work targeted the specific cities and industries Vending Vault serves; the site was trimmed from 29 pages to 15, cutting low-value SEO surface area. Hosting moved onto infrastructure the client controls. Dan also added a per-machine QR code so a customer can report an issue or request a product in under a minute, routed straight to the owner's inbox.

Outcome: A fast, mobile-friendly site the owner runs himself, leaner than the original build, plus a direct machine-to-owner feedback loop none of Vending Vault's local competitors offer. A follow-on operations audit (route planning, restocking) is a possible next phase.
