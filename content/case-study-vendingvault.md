# Vending Vault: A website the owner can actually run

**Client:** Vending Vault — full-service vending machine provider, Northern Colorado (Greeley, Fort Collins, Loveland, and surrounding towns)

## The challenge

Vending Vault's pitch is simple and honest: free installation, weekly service, no contracts, cancel anytime — a real differentiator in a market where most competitors lock customers into agreements. The web presence didn't reflect it. The old site was a stock WordPress install that was slow to update and did little to help Vending Vault get found by offices, gyms, apartments, and warehouses across Northern Colorado. And there was no way for a customer standing in front of a machine — jammed, or out of a favorite snack — to report it without hunting down a phone number.

## The approach

Dan built Vending Vault a new site from the ground up on Astro and Tailwind CSS, paired with Decap CMS — a git-based content system that lets the owner, Mark, edit photos and copy himself without touching code or waiting on a developer. Early SEO work targeted the specific cities and industries Vending Vault serves; as real priorities came into focus, the site was deliberately trimmed from 29 pages down to 15, cutting pages that added SEO surface area but no real value for visitors. Hosting later moved off Netlify onto infrastructure the client controls, with the CMS backed by GitLab authentication instead of a single vendor's login system. Dan also added a per-machine QR code feedback system: each machine got a unique code linking to a page pre-loaded with its photo and location, so a customer can report an issue, request a product, or leave feedback in under a minute — routed straight to the owner's inbox.

## The outcome

Vending Vault now has a fast, mobile-friendly site built around its real differentiators, plus a self-serve CMS the owner runs himself — no developer needed for a new photo or a copy change. The site is leaner than the original build, and the QR-code feedback loop gives Vending Vault something none of its local competitors offer: a direct line from the machine on the wall back to the owner. The website was delivered as its own scoped engagement; Dan and Vending Vault have since scoped a follow-on operations audit into route planning and restocking as a possible next phase.
