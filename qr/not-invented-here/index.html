About the name on your card

Every Deskless card is named for a ship from my favourite novels, Iain M. Banks's Culture books, where the AIs do the tedious work so people can do the interesting kind, and name themselves like they're in on a joke. I'd tell you this one is the best of the fifty, but I say that about all of them.

This one is the Not Invented Here, from Excession. Even the novel cannot agree on what class it is. The syndrome the name describes keeps teams rebuilding what they could adopt. Naming it is step one.

One of fifty cards, and this one found its way to you.
