Yours to run

Your team can update it

New photos, a new program description, a changed phone number: you make the update yourself in a few minutes from any browser. No developer needed, no waiting on us, no extra cost.

Fast

The page loads quickly even on a phone with weak signal, which is where a lot of families will first find you.

Safe

Nothing to patch and very little that can break. Inquiries go straight to you and nowhere else.

Readable by everyone

What we would ask you for

None of this has to be polished or complete. We can work with whatever you have right now.

  • Photos of your activities. The more real the better. Only ones where you have a photo release on file.
  • Your programs and waivers. The list, the way you would explain it to a new case manager at Foothills Gateway.
  • Service area and hours. Where you work and when people can reach you.
  • Logo and colours, if you have them. If not, we will put together a few options for you to choose from.
  • Who should get inquiries. The name and email address the contact form should go to.
